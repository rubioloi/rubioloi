# 🦥 Hi, I'm Iñigo Rubio

🎓 PhD student at Charles University and the Czech Academy of Sciences.

🌍 I study biodiversity patterns and ecological processes across spatial and
taxonomic scales. My research combines biogeography, ecology, and evolution
to understand how ecosystems are structured, how they change, and how
biodiversity is distributed across the planet.

My main interests include:

- 🌱 Biodiversity patterns across scales
- 🗺️ Biogeography and macroecology
- 🦠 Microbial and macroscopic biodiversity
- 🧬 Evolutionary ecology
- 📊 Reproducible workflows and ecological data analysis

I mainly work with R, spatial approaches, and scientific data visualization.
